## What the draft includes

- Terms covering the website, merchant app and hosted storefront service, with each user's relevant responsibilities.
- Free plan, 14-day Growth access, manual renewals and fallback to Free without deletion solely due to expiry.
- Paystack direct settlement after processing fees; no Purvey transaction commission and no sales-wallet withdrawal promise.
- Merchant obligations for products, delivery, returns, customer data and lawful store policies.
- A privacy notice distinguishing Purvey-controlled activities from merchant-managed customer/order records.
- Data categories, legal bases, provider categories, retention criteria, international processing, privacy rights and complaint contact.
- 180-day preference validity (the local-storage entry is not automatically erased on expiry), plus a clearly labelled account of planned optional tracking. New tools still require actual consent gating, accurate provider disclosures and testing before activation.
- No invented office, refund rate, guaranteed payout time, liability cap, arbitration venue, certification, DPO appointment or guaranteed security claim.

A privacy notice is not a substitute for a processor contract or technical compliance work.
