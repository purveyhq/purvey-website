# Privacy policy

**Owner-approved for publication on 1 November 2026. Legal review remains outstanding.**

What information Purvey handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Who we are and what this policy covers

Purvey is operated by Secern Technologies Limited, RC 7508148, Nigeria. You can contact us about privacy at [support@purveyhq.com](mailto:support@purveyhq.com).

This policy covers Purvey's marketing website and blog, merchant application, Purvey-hosted storefront services, and communications with us. It explains our handling of information relating to merchants, their authorised staff, customers and website visitors.

Some information is handled by a merchant or an independent provider for their own purposes. Their privacy notices also matter. The section below explains the distinction.

## Purvey's role and a merchant's role

For purposes such as operating merchant accounts, billing, responding to support requests and protecting the platform, Purvey determines why and how information is used. For those activities, we act as a data controller.

A merchant determines why customer and order information is collected for their store, how orders are fulfilled and whether customers receive the merchant's own marketing. For those activities, the merchant is responsible for the relevant privacy information and lawful processing, and Purvey processes records on the merchant's behalf to provide the platform.

Some activities, such as platform security and compliance with legal requests, may involve Purvey's own responsibilities. A provider such as Paystack may also process information for its own payment, fraud-prevention or legal purposes under its own privacy notice.

For a request about a store purchase or that merchant's marketing, contact the merchant first. You can also contact Purvey if you need help identifying the right route or your request concerns our processing.

## For merchants: account and business information

We receive account and business details from you and your authorised staff, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, sign-in credentials, business settings and staff permissions. We also handle product listings, images, stock records and the customer and order records you enter or receive.

For subscriptions and payment setup, we handle plan and billing history, payment references, amounts, payment and settlement status, bank details and any bank-ownership evidence requested.

Identity checks can involve your legal name, phone number, NIN or BVN and related identity information sent to Raptor. The current verification flow stores masked identifiers, verification results and supporting identity information. Full settlement account numbers are stored encrypted.

Public store details, product listings and published reviews are visible to other people. Keep private identity and bank information out of public content.

## For customers: shopping and order information

When you shop, we handle information needed for your order, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, items purchased, payment status and order history. Guest checkout also creates order and customer records. Customer accounts additionally use sign-in and account-verification information.

The merchant and authorised business members can access records needed to manage your order and provide service. Paystack handles supported online checkout; Purvey receives payment confirmations and transaction information. A merchant may separately share delivery details with its delivery service.

Reviews you submit may be published. Anyone you share an invoice or receipt link with may be able to view the document while that link is valid, so share it carefully.

Contact the merchant about its use of your information, its marketing or a store purchase. Contact [support@purveyhq.com](mailto:support@purveyhq.com) about Purvey’s handling of your information or if you need help directing a request.

## For everyone: website use and communications

We and the providers operating our services may receive technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device details, timestamps, session activity, errors and security events. We also handle privacy preferences and the messages or attachments you send to support.

Information comes from you, the merchant or customer involved, service activity, and payment or verification providers. Platform administrators may access relevant records for authorised support, security and operational purposes.

## Cookies and browser storage

Essential cookies and browser storage support sign-in, security, shopping carts, checkout, wishlists and interface preferences. Their use depends on the part of Purvey you visit. External font resources also load when pages are opened.

The marketing website remembers optional-cookie preferences in local storage. A saved choice is treated as valid for 180 days; the stored entry may remain in your browser until it is replaced or cleared. Rejecting optional technologies does not prevent browsing. You can change your choice through Cookie settings.

No optional advertising or analytics tags are currently connected in the inspected marketing-site or application code. Meta Pixel and other analytics tools are planned. Before introducing them, we will update the disclosures and provide the consent controls required for their use. Accepting optional cookies today only records your preference.

The marketing website’s preference controls do not manage essential application functions or an independent provider’s website. A signup email-marketing preference is separate from optional-cookie consent.

## Storage and international processing

Purvey uses service providers to operate its services. Some providers and their infrastructure may be outside Nigeria, so information may be stored or processed in other countries depending on the service involved.

International transfers are subject to applicable data-protection requirements, including the need for a lawful transfer basis and appropriate safeguards where required. Contact us if you need information about a particular processing arrangement or the safeguards applicable to your information.

## How long information is kept

Retention depends on the information and why it is needed. Account and store records support the ongoing service; order, payment and verification records also support transaction history, accounting, fraud prevention and disputes. Technical, security and support records support troubleshooting and service administration. Legal requirements may require longer retention.

There is no single automatic deletion period for all Purvey records. Subscription expiry, hiding a store, cancelling an order or removing a product from view does not necessarily erase the underlying records. The application currently has no self-service account-erasure feature.

For closure or an erasure request, contact [support@purveyhq.com](mailto:support@purveyhq.com). Requests require review of the records involved, merchant responsibilities, legal obligations and any applicable exceptions. Backup copies and independent provider records may follow separate retention arrangements; a change to a live record does not itself remove those copies.

## Security and your account

Protecting information forms part of operating Purvey. Access should be limited to the people and providers who need it for an authorised purpose. No website, network or method of storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Use a strong password, protect your sign-in details, review staff access, and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorised activity. Do not send passwords, full card details, NIN or BVN information through ordinary support email.

If a security incident requires notification under applicable law, the relevant notification requirements apply.

## Children and external websites

Purvey's merchant tools are intended for people with the legal capacity and authority to run or represent a business, rather than as a service directed at children. Merchants must consider the requirements applicable to their own products, customers and any information about children they handle. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been handled inappropriately through Purvey.

Links to a merchant's external pages, social platforms, payment pages or other independent services are subject to those services' own practices. Review their privacy information when using them.
